Age truncation and metapopulation viability (Heard 2024)

A reimplementation of the models of Heard, Scroggie, Hollanders and Scheele (2024), J Anim Ecol 93:1670–1683 (doi:10.1111/1365-2656.14177), for the green and golden bell frog (Litoria aurea) and the growling grass frog (L. raniformis).

Sources

The model follows the authors' R code and data (Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.10457229, CC BY 4.0) and the source of the R package steps 1.3.0 that they used, and was checked against the published paper. Carrying capacities, dispersal proportions and the dispersal cut-off are given only in the paper's Supplementary Material (not read); those compendium records are unverified.

Bd's role

Bd acts only through an imposed change in adult (and sub-adult) survival, from the pre-Bd estimate (museum specimens) to the post-Bd estimate (remnant populations). The models have no infection, load or transmission process.

Survival from age structure

Ages (lines of arrested growth) of 403 frogs, bundled in data/heard2024. Age − 1 is geometric with annual survival S (a catch curve, Scroggie 2012):

P(a) = (1 − S) S^a,   logit S = α + β₁ [L. raniformis] + β₂ [post-Bd] + u_site

with Normal site effects and the priors of the paper. The posterior means are 0.32 (post-Bd) against 0.65 (pre-Bd) for L. raniformis and 0.36 against 0.69 for L. aurea; the paper prints 0.318, 0.655, 0.352 and 0.688. The authors' simulation script enters 0.319, 0.641, 0.352 and 0.673; HeardParameters(...; survival = :as_printed) uses the printed values. Survival roughly halves.

Chytridiomycosis.HeardCatchCurve — Type
HeardCatchCurve(data = load_heard2024(); key = :heard2024)

Posterior of the Bayesian catch-curve model of Heard et al. (2024) (Scroggie 2012): the age a = LAG − 1 of each frog is geometric with annual survival S,

P(a) = (1 − S) S^a,   logit S = α + β₁ [L. raniformis] + β₂ [contemporary] + u_site

with site effects u ~ Normal(0, σ_site) and the priors of the authors' NIMBLE code (α, β ~ Normal(0, SD 31.6), σ_site ~ Uniform(0, 10), read from the compendium).

It implements the LogDensityProblems interface on the unconstrained vector [α, β₁, β₂, logit(σ_site/σ_max), u_1, …, u_K] (see parameter_names). solve(m; rng, chains = 3, warmup = 5000, iterations = 20_000, thin = 10) samples it by adaptive random-walk Metropolis and returns a HeardCatchCurveFit.

Spatial metapopulation

A female-only two-stage (sub-adult, adult) matrix model in every habitat cell of a 25 × 25 landscape of 65 m cells, with global environmental stochasticity, demographic stochasticity, exponential-kernel dispersal and a ceiling carrying capacity, run for 50 years over a grid of recruitment (mets), fecundity CV and mortality CV scenarios and 20 landscapes. The reimplementation reproduces the paper's extinction rates (82 % and 17 % of simulations for L. raniformis, 73 % and 13 % for L. aurea, post- and pre-Bd) and the logistic regression of Table 1 (fitted to our own simulations).

Quirks of `steps` kept by default

With as_coded = true the simulation keeps two behaviours of steps 1.3.0: a cell in the last column of the grid disperses from a neighbourhood shifted to column 0 of the next row, and after the ceiling the whole landscape is rounded by one multinomial draw, which re-allocates individuals among patches and stages every year. as_coded = false removes both.

Chytridiomycosis.HeardParameters — Type
HeardParameters(; adult_survival, carrying_capacity, prop_dispersing, mets, mets_cv,
                mortality_cv, subadult_survival = adult_survival, met_survival = 0.1,
                sex_ratio = 0.5, subadult_breeding_fraction = 0.2, initial_fraction = 0.5,
                time_steps = 50, cell_size = 65.0, distance_decay = 200.0,
                max_distance = 1500.0)
HeardParameters(:heard2024; species = :raniformis, epoch = :contemporary, mets = 24,
                mets_cv = 0.5, mortality_cv = 0.5, survival = :as_coded)

Parameters of the female-only, two-stage spatial metapopulation model of Heard et al. (2024) for Litoria aurea (species = :aurea) and L. raniformis (:raniformis):

  • adult_survival, subadult_survival: annual survival of adult and sub-adult females (set equal in the paper's simulations);
  • mets: metamorphs per adult female per year (scenario, 3–30), met_survival their survival to the sub-adult stage, sex_ratio the female fraction, and subadult_breeding_fraction the fraction of sub-adults that breed;
  • mets_cv: coefficient of variation of the fecundities (environmental stochasticity), mortality_cv: coefficient of variation of the mortality hazard (both scenarios, 0.1–1);
  • carrying_capacity: ceiling on sub-adult plus adult females per patch;
  • prop_dispersing: proportion of each stage dispersing per year;
  • cell_size, distance_decay, max_distance: landscape cell width and dispersal kernel exp(−d/distance_decay) truncated at max_distance (m);
  • initial_fraction, time_steps: each stage starts at round(K/2) × initial_fraction females per patch; the horizon is time_steps years.

The keyed constructor reads the species × epoch set (epoch = :historical for pre-Bd, :contemporary for post-Bd survival) from the compendium; mets, mets_cv and mortality_cv are scenario values the paper varies over a grid (see heard_scenario_grid). survival = :as_coded (default) uses the survival the authors' simulation script enters (0.319, 0.641, 0.352, 0.673 for L. raniformis post- and pre-Bd and L. aurea post- and pre-Bd); :as_printed uses the posterior means printed in the paper's Results (0.318, 0.655, 0.352, 0.688).

Bd acts only through adult_survival (an imposed change between epochs); the model has no infection process. The carrying capacities and dispersal proportions are given only in the paper's Supplementary Material and come from the authors' code (unverified records).

Chytridiomycosis.HeardMetapopulationProblem — Type
HeardMetapopulationProblem(p::HeardParameters, habitat::AbstractMatrix{Bool};
                           as_coded = true)
HeardMetapopulationProblem(p, landscape::Integer; as_coded = true)

One simulation of the Heard et al. (2024) spatial metapopulation on a grid of cells, where habitat[r, c] marks a habitat patch (an integer selects one of the 20 bundled landscapes, heard2024_landscapes). solve(prob; rng = Random.default_rng()) returns a HeardMetapopulationSolution.

Each year: growth with global environmental and full demographic stochasticity, kernel dispersal, then ceiling density dependence, as in the R package steps 1.3.0 that the authors used. With as_coded = true (default) two quirks of steps are kept: the shifted dispersal neighbourhood of last-column cells, and the landscape-wide multinomial re-allocation that steps uses to round populations after the ceiling is applied. See the source header for details.
