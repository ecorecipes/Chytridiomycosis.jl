Watering timing and extinction risk (Turner 2025)

A reimplementation of the stochastic population viability model of Turner et al. (2025), Conserv Sci Pract 7:e70022 (doi:10.1111/csp2.70022), for the southern bell frog Litoria raniformis in a regulated floodplain wetland that receives environmental water in August (~10 °C), September (~15 °C) or October (~20 °C).

Sources

The model follows the authors' R code (Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.10921748, CC BY 4.0) and was checked against the published paper, which describes it in outline.

Bd's role

The authors converted watering month into adult and metamorph survival through Bd load–survival relationships and stored the result as precomputed arrays. The code that produced them is not in the archive, and the paper describes the step without printing its regression coefficients or temperature series, so here Bd enters only through those survival series, which are bundled (data/turner2025).

Model

Six female age classes (eggs, then ages 1–5), annual step, 52 years. Each year the survivals, hatching, metamorphosis and fecundities are redrawn; density dependence acts on first-year, juvenile and adult survival and on egg laying; in a dry (unwatered) year there is no breeding. A replicate is extinct when no female aged 1 or more is left.

The reimplementation reproduces the authors' archived outputs (e.g. extinction 0.16, 0.06 and 0.04 for August, September and October with independent dry years, and the median minimum population sizes of all nine scenarios). The paper's values (0.13 and 0.05 for August and September; 0.025 and 0.0004 with at most two dry years; the quasi-extinction probabilities of its Fig. 3) are not reproduced, and the archived outputs do not reproduce them either: the paper's figures match a table hard-coded in the authors' script and appear to come from an earlier version of the code or arrays. October (0.04) agrees.

As coded

The authors' pop.func reads the survival arrays with the replicate count (10,000) as the row index, so every replicate uses the last replicate's survival series. That is the default here; pass the full array (load_turner2025_survival_array) to use one row per replicate. Wetness sequences, not archived as code, are drawn by turner_watering_sequence.

Chytridiomycosis.TurnerParameters — Type
TurnerParameters(:turner2025; as_printed = false)
TurnerParameters(; kwargs...)

Life history and density dependence of the Litoria raniformis population viability model of Turner et al. (2025), read from the compendium (the authors' code; the values the paper prints were checked against it): years, sex_ratio, fecundity (fecundity_mean, fecundity_sd, eggs per female aged 1–5), hatch_mean/hatch_sd, the beta distribution of survival from hatching to metamorphosis (tadpole_alpha, tadpole_beta), winter survival of first-years (winter_daily_survival^winter_duration), the egg ceiling (spawn_capacity × clutch_size_mean × sex_ratio), the density references tadpole_capacity, juvenile_capacity, adult_capacity with their caps, the three fitted multiplier curves (egg_a/b/c, tadpole_a/b/c, juvenile_a/b/c) and p_wet, the probability that a year is watered. Bd-dependent survival is not a parameter: see TurnerPVAProblem. as_printed = true uses the paper's mean clutch size (3244) instead of the coded 3245.

Chytridiomycosis.turner_watering_sequence — Function
turner_watering_sequence(rng, pattern, years; p_wet = 0.7) -> BitVector

A sequence of watered (true) and dry years: year 1 is watered and each later year with probability p_wet; with pattern = :max2dry (:max1dry) a year is forced wet after two (one) consecutive dry years, :random has no cap. This rule is inferred from the archived arrays of Turner et al. (2025); the code that drew them is not archived.

Chytridiomycosis.TurnerPVAProblem — Type
TurnerPVAProblem(p::TurnerParameters; survival, start, pattern = :random,
                 wetness = nothing, replicates = 10_000, as_coded = true)
TurnerPVAProblem(:turner2025; month = :august, pattern = :random, replicates = 10_000,
                 survival_array = nothing)

The stochastic age-classified population viability model of Turner et al. (2025) for Litoria raniformis under one environmental-watering scenario. Inputs:

  • survival: a years × 4 matrix (adult survival mean, SD, metamorph summer survival mean, SD per year) shared by all replicates (the authors' code as run), or a replicates × years × 5 array with wetness in slice 1 (one row per replicate, as intended; as_coded = false);
  • start: a 6 × k matrix of candidate start populations (eggs, ages 1–5), resampled with replacement from the non-empty ones;
  • wetness: a replicates × years BitMatrix of watered years; nothing draws them with turner_watering_sequence and pattern.

The keyed constructor loads the bundled scenario (load_turner2025); pass the full array from load_turner2025_survival_array as survival_array to use per-replicate survival and the authors' own wetness sequences.

solve(prob; rng = Random.default_rng()) returns a TurnerPVASolution. Bd enters only through the precomputed survival series.
