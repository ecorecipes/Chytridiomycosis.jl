Solving problems

Every problem is solved with solve (from CommonSolve.jl). Those that lower to one ODE can also be converted directly:

Chytridiomycosis.to_ode_problem — Function
to_ode_problem(prob)

The SciMLBase.ODEProblem that solve(prob) integrates, for the problems that lower to a single ODE: SchmidtSLIProblem, BriggsLoadProblem, LoadTippingProblem, LoadTipping (one host, pool fixed), BsalLarvalProblem, MultiHostPoolProblem (with its inoculation callback), ZoosporePersistenceProblem, RefugiaODEProblem, SeasonalBsalProblem (with its inflow switch times as tstops), TransmissionSLIProblem, SusceptibleChainProblem, CohabitationProblem, DrawertODEProblem, BsalStageTemperatureProblem, DoddingtonODEProblem (with its overwintering, ageing and introduction callbacks), OverlappingHabitatProblem and LoucaDOIProblem (method of lines over DOI bins, with exposure, disturbance and end-of-run callbacks). Use it to solve with other options or to pass the model to other SciML tools.
