Nested and stochastic models

New models

These models are new work in this package, not published results.

One host–pathogen–microbiome model in which each piece of biology is a switch, anchored on Schmidt et al. (2017), and its stochastic version. Both draw on one list of transitions, so they cannot disagree.

Chytridiomycosis.ChytridBiology — Type
ChytridBiology(; latent = false, environment = false, load = false, recovery = false,
    larvae = false, larval_infection = false, carry_over = 0, ...)
ChytridBiology(:wang2021; kwargs...)

Which pieces of biology are switched on in NestedChytridProblem, and the values they take when on: environmental_fraction (share of transmission through the pool) and pool_half_life (yr); light_shedding (shedding of a light infection relative to a heavy one) and progression (light to heavy, yr⁻¹); recovery_rate (yr⁻¹); larval_period (yr), larval_death (yr⁻¹), adult_to_larva (ha yr⁻¹) and carry_over. The keyed constructor takes carry_over from the compendium.

fadeout_density (per ha; 0 switches it off) is a fade-out rule. Once the pathogen has been introduced, if infected animals plus the pool's equivalent in shedding animals fall below it, they are set to zero; if all animals (adults and larvae) fall below it, the host population is set to zero.

Chytridiomycosis.NestedChytridProblem — Type
NestedChytridProblem(p::SchmidtParameters, biology::ChytridBiology,
    treatment = MicrobiomeTreatment(); treat = :adults, coverage = 0, treat_times = [0.0],
    retention = 0, t_intro = 0.0, I0 = 0.01, tspan = (0.0, 10.0))

One host–pathogen–microbiome model with each piece of biology switched on or off by biology. The population starts at its disease-free state; at each of treat_times a fraction coverage of the untreated animals in treat (:adults, :larvae, :both) is treated; I0 infectious adults per ha arrive at t_intro. Time in years. Treated animals pass the bacteria on within their stage through treatment.direct; there is no environmental reservoir of bacteria, so treatment.environmental must be 0.

solve returns series t, adults, infected (adults in any infected class) and min_adults, mean_adults, final_adults (fractions of K, after t_intro), final_prevalence and final_treated; and, for the fade-out rule, pathogen_fadeout and host_extinction (the times they happened, or NaN).

Chytridiomycosis.invasion_growth_rate — Function
invasion_growth_rate(p::SchmidtParameters, biology::ChytridBiology)

Initial growth rate (yr⁻¹) of the pathogen in an untreated host population at its disease-free state: the largest real part of the eigenvalues of the infected classes' Jacobian, found by finite differences. Positive means the pathogen invades.

Chytridiomycosis.StochasticNestedProblem — Type
StochasticNestedProblem(p::SchmidtParameters, biology::ChytridBiology,
    treatment = MicrobiomeTreatment(); area = 20.0, introduced = 1, dt = 1/1460,
    saveat = 1/52, kwargs...)

Stochastic version of NestedChytridProblem: whole animals in area hectares, starting at the disease-free state (rounded), with introduced infectious adults arriving at t_intro. Other keywords (treat, coverage, treat_times, retention, t_intro, tspan) are as for NestedChytridProblem; at a treatment each untreated animal is treated with probability coverage. biology.fadeout_density is ignored: extinction happens by itself.

solve(prob; rng) returns one run: series t, adults, infected (animals of any stage in an infected class), and mean_adults and final_adults (fractions of the starting number of adults, after t_intro), peak_infected, pathogen_extinction and host_extinction (times, or NaN).
