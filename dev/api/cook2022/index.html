Treatment of metamorphs and population projection (Cook 2022)

This is a reference implementation of the stage-structured population projection of Cook et al. (2022), Conserv Sci Pract 4:e12762 (doi:10.1111/csp2.12762). The study treated Cascades frog (Rana cascadae) metamorphs with itraconazole, which raised their apparent overwinter survival from 0.11 to 0.48. The projection asks what this does to population growth. The model is described only in the Supporting Information ("Population simulations", Figure S3, Table S2), and every value comes from the compendium.

Model

Each time step is one year. The stages are metamorphs, young of the previous year (YOPY), subadults and adults.

  • Metamorphs. They survive their first winter with probability SU if untreated or ST if treated (posterior means 0.12 and 0.49).
  • YOPY. They survive with probability SY = 0.8 SA.
  • Subadults. They survive with probability SS = SA. Survivors mature with probability GS = 0.725.
  • Adults. They survive with probability SA.
  • Reproduction. Adult females (a fraction PF = 0.52 of adults) each produce Poisson(F) metamorphs.

Each year, SA and F are drawn from the 28 population-year estimates of Table S2 (environmental stochasticity). All transitions are binomial (demographic stochasticity). A simulation starts from 1500 metamorphs, 50 YOPY, 50 subadults and 100 adults and runs for 10 years. Its λ is the geometric mean of the annual adult growth rates. The paper reports the mean over 1000 simulations.

Choices where the SI is silent

The authors' code was not found. Each choice below has a switch in CookProjectionProblem or CookParameters.

  • The metamorphs of a year come from the adults present that year, after overwinter survival (breeding = :after_survival).
  • Surviving subadults mature with probability GS.
  • SA and F are drawn from the same population-year (paired = true).
  • SU and ST are fixed at their posterior means, because the posteriors are not published.
  • Treatment years are a fixed share of the 10 years, placed at random (schedule = :fixed).

Results

  • Untreated. Mean λ is 0.94, which reproduces the published 0.9.
  • 40% of metamorphs treated in 40% of years. Mean λ is 1.02, above 1 as published. Lowering either share to 20% gives λ < 1.
  • All metamorphs treated every year. Mean λ is 1.30, against the published 1.2. This target is discrepant. Breeding before survival gives 0.97 and 1.25 instead.

The projection matrix of mean rates (cook_projection_matrix) supports sensitivity and elasticity analysis. The paper prints neither, and the matrix's λ is not a published number.

Chytridiomycosis.CookParameters — Type
CookParameters(; SU, ST, adult_survival, fecundity, yopy_ratio, growth, female_fraction,
               paired = true)
CookParameters(:cook2022; paired = true)

Parameters of the Cascades frog projection of Cook et al. (2022), SI Table S2:

  • SU, ST: overwinter survival of untreated and itraconazole-treated metamorphs. Each is a number, or a Distributions distribution sampled once a year.
  • adult_survival: the pool of annual adult (and subadult) survival probabilities, SA = SS.
  • fecundity: the pool of metamorphs produced per adult female, F.
  • yopy_ratio: YOPY survival relative to adult survival (SY = yopy_ratio × SA).
  • growth: annual probability that a surviving subadult becomes an adult (GS).
  • female_fraction: proportion of adults that are female (PF).
  • paired: draw SA and F from the same population-year (true) or independently.

The keyed constructor reads every value from the compendium (SU = 0.12 and ST = 0.49, the posterior means used in the projection).

Chytridiomycosis.CookProjectionProblem — Type
CookProjectionProblem(p::CookParameters; treated_fraction = 0, treated_years = 0,
    years = 10, u0 = nothing, breeding = :after_survival, schedule = :fixed)

A stochastic projection of the Cook et al. (2022) model, SI Fig. S3: metamorph → YOPY → subadult → adult, with adults producing metamorphs.

  • treated_fraction: the proportion of metamorphs treated in a treatment year.
  • treated_years: the proportion of years with treatment (both 0 without treatment, both 1 for "treatment of all metamorphs in each year").
  • u0: the initial (metamorph, yopy, subadult, adult) counts. The default is read from the compendium: 1500, 50, 50 and 100.
  • breeding and schedule select the choices described in the source header: :after_survival or :before_survival, and :fixed or :random.

solve(prob; nsims = 1000, rng) runs the simulations and returns a CookProjectionSolution.

CookProjectionSolution

Result of solve(::CookProjectionProblem):

  • problem;
  • states: a (years + 1) × 4 × nsims array of stage counts (metamorph, YOPY, subadult, adult);
  • λ: the growth rate of each simulation, (A_T / A_0)^(1/T), which is the geometric mean of its annual adult growth rates.

Use cook_growth_rate for the mean and the proportion of simulations with λ > 1.

Chytridiomycosis.cook_projection_matrix — Function
cook_projection_matrix(p::CookParameters; treated = 0.0, breeding = :after_survival)
    -> Matrix{Float64}

The 4 × 4 projection matrix of mean rates (stages metamorph, YOPY, subadult, adult), with the mean of the survival and fecundity pools and a fraction treated of metamorphs treated each year. With breeding = :after_survival, metamorphs come from the adults after survival and maturation, so the fecundity terms are PF E[F SA] (adults) and PF E[F SA] GS (subadults). E[F SA] is the mean product when paired, and the product of the means otherwise. population_growth_rate, sensitivity_matrix and elasticity_matrix apply to it.

The paper reports only the stochastic simulation. The matrix ignores environmental and demographic variance, so its dominant eigenvalue is larger than the mean simulated λ. It is provided for sensitivity analysis and is not a published result.
