Overlapping habitats and dilution (Espira et al. 2022)

This is a reference implementation of the two-species environmental transmission model of Espira et al. (2022), Am Nat 199:E43–E56 (doi:10.1086/717413). It was checked against the published version, the author manuscript (PMC9136953; same equations) and the online supplement (eqs S1–S18, Figs S1–S2). The paper is analytical: its figures use no printed parameter values, and its results are checked as statements on random parameter sets. The only numbers are the supplement's worked example (Table S1), which is in the compendium.

Each species has susceptible, infectious and recovered hosts and sheds pathogens into its own habitat, of area ε_i. A fraction θ_i of habitat i is shared with the other species. There are no births, deaths or pathogen growth.

dS_i/dt = −κ_i ρ_i π_i S_i (E_i + θ_i E_j)
dI_i/dt =  κ_i ρ_i π_i S_i (E_i + θ_i E_j) − γ_i I_i
dR_i/dt =  γ_i I_i
dE_i/dt =  α_i I_i/ε_i − E_i (κ_i ρ_i N_i/ε_i + θ_j κ_j ρ_j N_j/ε_i + μ)

The pick-up efficiency η_i = a_i/(a_i + μ), with a_i = κ_i ρ_i N_i/ε_i, places each species between density-dependent-like (η → 0) and frequency-dependent-like (η → 1) transmission. Adding species 2 dilutes when the community reproduction number R0,C falls below species 1's own R0,1.

Corrections to the paper
  • Eq. 6 is a misprint. The printed closed form of R0,C (eq. 6, and eq. 7 in the same form, in both the published version and the manuscript) has μ without ε_i, and θ₁ where eq. 1 gives θ₂ in the first denominator. The supplement's own S12, S15 and S16 have μ ε_i and θ_j, which is the next-generation matrix of eq. 1. community_reproduction_number computes S15; as_printed = true evaluates eq. 6 as printed. The two agree only when ε₁ = ε₂ = 1.
  • Eq. 8 needs consistent overlaps. The dilution criterion of eq. 8 (and S17) agrees with R0,C < R0,1 exactly when θ₁ε₁ = θ₂ε₂, as the definition of θ implies.
  • Figs S1 and S2 (habitat 1 inside habitat 2, and the reverse) are printed with the same heat-map raster as Fig. 4. On the figure axes R0,C/R0,1 depends on θ₁ and θ₂ separately, so the three cannot coincide: S1 differs from Fig. 4 by up to 0.52 and S2 by up to 0.17. With habitat 1 inside habitat 2, dilution grows stronger, not weaker, as the overlap shrinks.
  • Worked example. Its text uses κ₂ρ₂ = 0.0002 (Table S1: 0.0001) and computes 4 × 2 = 4 for the decay term.

Reproduced: R0,C ≥ min(R0,1, R0,2); R0,C = max(R0,1, R0,2) without overlap (eq. 10); the complete-overlap form (eq. 11); the dilution criteria of eqs 8 and 9; and the attenuation of dilution and amplification as species 1's pick-up efficiency rises (Fig. 3) and as the overlap shrinks (Fig. 4).

Chytridiomycosis.EspiraParameters — Type
EspiraParameters(; N, γ, κ, ρ, π, α, ε, θ, μ)

Parameters of the two-species environmental transmission model of Espira et al. (2022), eq. 1. Every argument but μ is a pair, one value per species:

  • N: population size;
  • γ: recovery rate (day⁻¹);
  • κ: contact rate with the environment (contacts day⁻¹);
  • ρ: pick-up, the area covered per contact times the fraction of pathogens picked up there;
  • π: probability of infection per pathogen picked up;
  • α: shedding rate (pathogens day⁻¹ per infectious host);
  • ε: area of the species' habitat;
  • θ: habitat overlap, θ_i = area(E_i ∩ E_j)/area(E_i), in [0, 1]. By this definition θ_1 ε_1 = θ_2 ε_2; the constructor does not enforce it, since eq. 1 does not need it.
  • μ: pathogen decay rate (day⁻¹), the same in both habitats.

The paper is analytical and its figures use no printed parameter values; the only numbers are the worked example of the supplement (Table S1, in the compendium under :espira2022), which has no recovery or shedding rates. There is therefore no keyed constructor; see espira_parameters_from_efficiencies for the axes of Figs 3 and 4.

Chytridiomycosis.pickup_efficiency — Function
pickup_efficiency(p::EspiraParameters) -> (η₁, η₂)

The pathogen pick-up efficiency of each species (Espira et al. 2022, eq. 4), η_i = (κ_i ρ_i N_i/ε_i)/(κ_i ρ_i N_i/ε_i + μ): the fraction of the pathogens a species sheds that it picks up itself before they decay, when alone. Near 0 transmission is density-dependent-like, near 1 frequency-dependent-like.

Chytridiomycosis.species_reproduction_numbers — Method
species_reproduction_numbers(p::EspiraParameters) -> [R0,1, R0,2]

The single-species basic reproduction numbers of Espira et al. (2022), eq. 5: R0,i = (π_i α_i/γ_i) η_i, the reproduction number of species i alone (N_j = 0).

Chytridiomycosis.next_generation_matrix — Method
next_generation_matrix(p::EspiraParameters; reduced = true)

The next-generation matrix of Espira et al. (2022), eq. 1, at the disease-free state (S_i = N_i). With reduced = true it is the 2 × 2 host-to-host matrix K, where K[j, i] is the number of species-j infections caused, through the environment, by one species-i infection. With reduced = false it is F V⁻¹ on the infected states [I₁, I₂, E₁, E₂]. Both have spectral radius community_reproduction_number.

Chytridiomycosis.community_reproduction_number — Function
community_reproduction_number(p::EspiraParameters; as_printed = false)

The community basic reproduction number R0,C of Espira et al. (2022): the spectral radius of the next-generation matrix of eq. 1 (next_generation_matrix), in closed form

R0,C = (K₁₁ + K₂₂)/2 + sqrt((K₁₁ + K₂₂)² - 4 K₁₁ K₂₂ (1 - θ₁θ₂))/2,
K_ii = (π_i α_i/γ_i) (κ_iρ_iN_i/ε_i)/(κ_iρ_iN_i/ε_i + θ_jκ_jρ_jN_j/ε_i + μ).

This is eq. S15 of the supplement. as_printed = true evaluates eq. 6 of the main text exactly as printed, whose K_ii denominators are κ_iρ_iN_i + θ_iκ_jρ_jN_j + μ: μ without ε_i, and the other overlap parameter. S15 shows these are misprints; the two agree when ε₁ = ε₂ = 1 and θ₁ = θ₂.

Chytridiomycosis.dilution_threshold — Function
dilution_threshold(p::EspiraParameters)

The right-hand side of the dilution criterion of Espira et al. (2022), eq. 8:

[(κ₂ρ₂N₂/ε₂ + θ₁κ₁ρ₁N₁/ε₂ + θ₂μ)/(κ₂ρ₂N₂/ε₂ + θ₁κ₁ρ₁N₁/ε₂ + μ)] R0,2/R0,1.

Adding species 2 dilutes (R0,C < R0,1) if and only if η₂ exceeds it. When habitat 2 lies inside habitat 1 (θ₂ = 1) the factor is 1 (eq. 9).

source
Chytridiomycosis.dilution_effect — Function
dilution_effect(p::EspiraParameters; criterion = :R0) -> Bool

Whether adding species 2 to species 1 lowers the epidemic potential, R0,C < R0,1 (Espira et al. 2022). criterion = :R0 compares community_reproduction_number with R0,1 directly; criterion = :eq8 tests η₂ >dilution_threshold(p), the paper's eq. 8.

Chytridiomycosis.espira_parameters_from_efficiencies — Function
espira_parameters_from_efficiencies(η₁, η₂, r; θ₂ = 1, ε = (1, 1), μ = 1)
    -> EspiraParameters

Parameters on the axes of Espira et al. (2022), Figs 3 and 4: pick-up efficiencies η₁, η₂ (in [0, 1)), ratio of epidemic potentials r = R0,2/R0,1, and overlap θ₂ (the fraction of species 2's habitat shared with species 1, the rows of Fig. 4), with the habitat areas ε and the decay rate μ fixed as in the figures. θ₁ = θ₂ ε₂/ε₁ follows from the definition of θ. Fig. 3 (complete overlap) is θ₂ = 1 with ε₁ = ε₂.

The paper prints no values. This constructor fixes N = ρ = 1, γ = α = 1 and π₁ = 1, sets κ_i ε_i⁻¹ = μ η_i/(1 - η_i) so that eq. 4 gives η_i, and π₂ so that eq. 5 gives r; R0,C/R0,1 depends only on these quantities.

Chytridiomycosis.OverlappingHabitatProblem — Type
OverlappingHabitatProblem(p::EspiraParameters; I0 = (1, 0), E0 = (0, 0),
                          tspan = (0.0, 365.0))

The epidemic model of Espira et al. (2022), eq. 1, in days, on the state [S₁, S₂, I₁, I₂, R₁, R₂, E₁, E₂]. Initially S_i = N_i - I0_i, R_i = 0 and the pathogen concentrations are E0. The depletion of the environment uses the constant N_i, as eq. 1 does. solve(prob) returns the ODESolution; to_ode_problem gives the ODEProblem.
