Bsal with an infectable larval stage

The Schmidt et al. (2017) model with larvae that can carry infection through metamorphosis (carry-over from Wang et al. 2021).

Chytridiomycosis.BsalLarvalStage — Type
BsalLarvalStage(; carry_over, larval_period = 0.33, larval_death = 1.0,
                adult_to_larva = 0, larva_to_larva = 0, larva_to_adult = 0)
BsalLarvalStage(:wang2021; kwargs...)

A larval stage for the Schmidt Bsal model. carry_over is the probability that an infected larva is still infected after metamorphosis (the keyed constructor takes it from the compendium: 5 of 10 larvae in Wang et al. 2021). larval_period (yr) and larval_death (yr⁻¹) describe the stage. The transmission rates (ha yr⁻¹, like Schmidt's β) are from infectious adults to larvae, between larvae, and from infected larvae to adults.

source
Chytridiomycosis.BsalLarvalProblem — Type
BsalLarvalProblem(p::SchmidtParameters, stage; I0 = 0.01, tspan = (0.0, 10.0))

Schmidt's Bsal model with the larval stage, started at the disease-free equilibrium with I0 infectious adults per ha. Time in years.

solve returns series t, adults, larvae, infectious (adults), larval_prevalence, and min_adults, mean_adults and final_adults as fractions of K.

source
Chytridiomycosis.disease_free_larvae — Function

Larvae per ha at the disease-free equilibrium, b K × larval_period.

source
Chytridiomycosis.larval_reproduction_number — Function
larval_reproduction_number(p, s)

Infected larvae produced by one infected larva among uninfected larvae, by larva-to-larva transmission alone: larva_to_larva × W* / (m + μ_W). Above 1, Bsal can persist in the larvae without any adult.

Toads with infection after metamorphosis

The Mitchell et al. (2008) model with surviving infected metamorphs and adult carriers.

Chytridiomycosis.PostMetamorphicInfection — Type
PostMetamorphicInfection(; metamorph_survival = 0, metamorph_exposure_days = 0,
    adult_exposure_days = 0, adult_relative_susceptibility = 1,
    adult_relative_shedding = 0, adult_clearance = 0, adult_disease_death = 0)

Infection after metamorphosis for the Mitchell toad model. metamorph_survival is the probability that an infected metamorph clears Bd and survives (0 in Mitchell et al. 2008). Uninfected metamorphs are exposed to the pond for metamorph_exposure_days as they leave. Adults are at the pond for adult_exposure_days from the start of breeding, are infected at adult_relative_susceptibility times the tadpole rate, shed at adult_relative_shedding times the tadpole rate while there, clear infection at adult_clearance and die of it at adult_disease_death (both yr⁻¹).

source
Chytridiomycosis.ToadStageProblem — Type
ToadStageProblem(p::MitchellParameters, stages::PostMetamorphicInfection; years = 35,
    spinup = 100, Z0 = 1e4, A0 = p.K / 2, toad_limit = 50.0, bd_limit = 100.0)

The Mitchell et al. (2008) pond model with infection after metamorphosis (stages). Set up and extinction limits are as in MitchellPondProblem, except that zoospores are not set to zero at breeding while infected adults that shed are present.

solve returns series at each event (t, A, A_infected, Z), toads_persist, bd_persist (zoospores above the limit, or shedding adults still infected), A_disease_free, A_final, A_mean (adults over the last 10 years) and adult_prevalence (the highest infected fraction of adults in the last 10 years, taken when adults leave the pond).
