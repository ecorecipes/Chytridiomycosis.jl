Zoospore persistence (Rumschlag et al. 2022)

This is a reference implementation of the zoospore persistence model of Rumschlag et al. (2022), J Anim Ecol 91:170–181 (doi:10.1111/1365-2656.13612). Its equations and values were checked against the published version. The bioRxiv preprint (doi:10.1101/2021.03.17.435818) prints the same numbers.

The refit uses the authors' deposited assay data (figshare, doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.16814701, CC BY 4.0), bundled in data/rumschlag2022. It reproduces the paper's estimates:

  • the lifespans 14.4, 13.3, 11.4 and 9.4 h;
  • the persistence time of 21.6 h;
  • their "± 95% CI" values;
  • the likelihood ratio χ² of 7.581;
  • 13 of the 15 AICc values in Table 1.

Model

Bd zoospores were followed in four waters at 21 °C: artificial spring water (ASW) and water from three Florida ponds (Trout Creek, Flatwoods, Green Swamp). Cuban treefrog tadpoles were the hosts. Time is in days, and zoospores are counted per µl.

dZL/dt = −m ZL           live zoospores
dZD/dt =  m ZL − d ZD    dead zoospores
dS/dt  = −β S ZL         susceptible tadpoles
dI/dt  =  β S ZL         infected tadpoles

The paper reports the lifespan 24/m and the persistence time 24/d in hours.

Two corrections to the paper
  • Eq. 6. As printed, eq. 6 has its bracket reversed and is negative for every t > 0. pool_state uses the correct solution.
  • The scale of β. The paper gives no units for β. The data identify only the product β ZL(0). The printed β = 7.1 × 10⁻⁶ equals that product divided by 10⁵, so it reproduces the paper's Fig. 4 only with ZL0 = 1e5 in infection_probability. That is an inferred convention.
Chytridiomycosis.RumschlagParameters — Type
RumschlagParameters(; m, d, β)
RumschlagParameters(:rumschlag2022; water = :ASW)

Parameters of the Rumschlag et al. (2022) zoospore persistence model for one water source:

  • m: per capita death rate of live zoospores (day⁻¹);
  • d: per capita decomposition rate of dead zoospores (day⁻¹);
  • β: per capita transmission rate (day⁻¹ per unit of the zoospore concentration used for ZL0).

The keyed constructor reads the paper's selected model (four water-specific m, one d, one β) from the compendium. water is one of :ASW (artificial spring water), :TroutCreek, :Flatwoods or :GreenSwamp.

  • Rates. The paper reports lifespans in hours. The constructor converts them with the units layer: m = 1/lifespan and d = 1/persistence, per day.
  • β. β is the printed value, 7.1 × 10⁻⁶, which has no stated units. A refit to the deposited data shows that it equals β ZL0 / 10⁵, so it reproduces the paper's prevalence (Fig. 4) only with ZL0 = 1e5 in infection_probability. That convention is inferred, not printed.
  • Refitted β. A β refitted by maximum_likelihood is in µl zoospore⁻¹ day⁻¹. It goes with the fitted initial concentration (about 143 zoospores µl⁻¹).

Chytridiomycosis.ZoosporePersistenceProblem — Type
ZoosporePersistenceProblem(p::RumschlagParameters; ZL0, ZD0 = 0, S0 = 1, I0 = 0,
                           tspan = (0.0, 2.0))

The four-state model of Rumschlag et al. (2022), eqs 1–4, in days:

dZL/dt = −m ZL
dZD/dt =  m ZL − d ZD
dS/dt  = −β S ZL
dI/dt  =  β S ZL

ZL0 has no default. It is the initial live zoospore concentration, on the scale that β is per (see RumschlagParameters). For the survival assay it is in zoospores µl⁻¹. The paper fits it but prints no value; its Fig. 3a and the refit put it near 143 µl⁻¹. solve(prob) returns the ODESolution of the state [ZL, ZD, S, I]. Use to_ode_problem for the ODEProblem. To follow the infection assay, in which a tadpole is added at lag t1 and removed one day later, start from ZL0 = ZL(0) exp(−m t1) with tspan = (0, 1), or use infection_probability.

Chytridiomycosis.infection_probability — Function
infection_probability(p::RumschlagParameters, ZL0, t1; τ = 1.0)

Probability that a tadpole is infected when it is added t1 days after ZL0 live zoospores and removed τ days later (Rumschlag et al. 2022, eq. 10; τ = 1 day in the paper):

p = 1 − exp(β ZL0 exp(−m t1) (exp(−m τ) − 1)/m)

ZL0 must be on the scale that β is per. With the compendium's β use ZL0 = 1e5, the paper's inferred convention. For example, infection_probability(RumschlagParameters(:rumschlag2022), 1e5, 0.0) gives the paper's Fig. 4a prevalence in artificial spring water at lag 0 (about 0.29). With a refitted β, use the fitted ZL0 (zoospores µl⁻¹).

A live/dead zoospore pool for other models

The live and dead compartments form a reusable component. A qPCR or eDNA count sees DNA from live and dead zoospores (ZL + ZD), but only live zoospores infect (ZL). Other models can add this pool to their own right-hand side through pool_derivatives.

Chytridiomycosis.ZoosporePool — Type
ZoosporePool(m, d)

A free-living zoospore pool with live and dead compartments:

dZL/dt = shedding − m ZL − uptake
dZD/dt = m ZL − d ZD

m is the per capita death rate of live zoospores and d the per capita decomposition rate of dead ones, both in the host model's time unit. A qPCR or eDNA assay detects DNA from both compartments, so it sees ZL + ZD (qpcr_zoospores); only ZL can infect (infectious_zoospores). The structure is that of Rumschlag et al. (2022), eqs 1–2; ZoosporePool(p::RumschlagParameters) builds the pool of one water source.

Use pool_derivatives inside another model's right-hand side, and pool_state for the closed-form solution without shedding.

Time derivatives of the live (ZL) and dead (ZD) zoospores of pool, with shedding live zoospores added and uptake live zoospores removed (for example by infection) per unit time.

source
Chytridiomycosis.pool_state — Function
pool_state(pool::ZoosporePool, ZL0, t; ZD0 = 0) -> (ZL, ZD)

Live and dead zoospores at time t, starting from ZL0 live and ZD0 dead, with no shedding or uptake:

ZL(t) = ZL0 exp(−m t)
ZD(t) = ZD0 exp(−d t) + ZL0 m/(d − m) (exp(−m t) − exp(−d t))

The case d = m is the limit ZL0 m t exp(−m t). With ZD0 = 0 this is Rumschlag et al. (2022) eqs 5–6, with the sign of eq. 6 corrected: as printed (in both the preprint and the published version) the bracket is exp(−d t) − exp(−m t), which is negative for every t > 0.

source
Chytridiomycosis.qpcr_zoospores — Function
qpcr_zoospores(ZL, ZD)
qpcr_zoospores(pool::ZoosporePool, ZL0, t; ZD0 = 0)

What a DNA-based count (qPCR or eDNA) of the pool sees: live plus dead zoospores, ZL + ZD. It assumes that a dead zoospore keeps its DNA until it decomposes and that live and dead zoospores carry the same DNA; neither assumption was tested by Rumschlag et al. (2022), who counted live and dead cells by Trypan blue staining. The second form evaluates the closed-form pool of pool_state at time t.

Chytridiomycosis.viable_fraction — Function
viable_fraction(ZL, ZD)
viable_fraction(pool::ZoosporePool, t)

The fraction of the DNA-detectable pool that is alive, ZL/(ZL + ZD). The second form starts from live zoospores only (ZD0 = 0). It does not depend on ZL0. It falls from 1 at t = 0. When d > m it tends to 1 − m/d; otherwise it tends to 0.

Data and refit

Reproduction

The refit reproduces a published analysis; it is not a new model. Survival times are the recorded times of the counts, because Table 1 is not reproduced with the nominal times. One initial concentration ZL0 is fitted and shared by both assays. That gives the paper's degrees of freedom.

RumschlagCount(water, t, live, dead)

One unit of the Rumschlag et al. (2022) zoospore survival assay: water source water (one of :ASW, :TroutCreek, :Flatwoods, :GreenSwamp), sampling time t (days since the zoospores were added), and live and dead zoospores counted by Trypan blue (cells per µl).

Chytridiomycosis.load_rumschlag2022 — Function
load_rumschlag2022(; time = :exact) -> (survival, infection)

The two assays of Rumschlag et al. (2022): a Vector{RumschlagCount} (199 units) and a Vector{RumschlagTadpole} (197 tadpoles). Pond codes are mapped as in the deposit's metadata (A = Trout Creek, B = Flatwoods, C = Green Swamp). Survival times are the recorded time of each count (time.hrs, 0 to 50 h) with time = :exact, or the nominal sampling times (0, 6, 16, 24, 48 h) with time = :nominal. All times are returned in days. The paper's Table 1 is reproduced with the recorded times, not the nominal ones.

source
Chytridiomycosis.RumschlagModel — Type
RumschlagModel(; m = 4, d = 1, β = 1, data = load_rumschlag2022(), τ = 1.0)

Likelihood of the Rumschlag et al. (2022) assays (paper eqs 7 and 10) for one of the 15 models of their Table 1. m, d and β give the number of estimates of each:

  • 1: one estimate shared by all four waters;
  • 2: one for artificial spring water and one for the three ponds together;
  • 4: one per water.

In Table 1, every parameter that is not shared has the same number of estimates (2 or 4). The default is the paper's selected model. τ is the exposure time (days).

The free parameters, all on the log scale, are listed by parameter_names:

  • the rates m and d (day⁻¹);
  • β (µl zoospore⁻¹ day⁻¹);
  • ZL0, the initial live zoospores (µl⁻¹), shared by both assays;
  • SD_L and SD_D, the standard deviations of the log counts.

The model implements the LogDensityProblems interface. The log density is the log-likelihood, with a flat prior on the log parameters. Fit it with maximum_likelihood.

Chytridiomycosis.rumschlag_model_selection — Function
rumschlag_model_selection(; data = load_rumschlag2022(), kwargs...) -> Vector{NamedTuple}

Fit the 15 models of Rumschlag et al. (2022) Table 1 and rank them by AICc. The models are all the ways of giving each of m, d and β one shared estimate, except that the parameters that vary all use the same split (2 or 4). Keywords are passed to maximum_likelihood.

Each entry has the fields of the fit, plus k (estimates of m, d and β), Δaicc and weight (the Akaike weight). Entries are sorted by AICc.

source
Chytridiomycosis.rumschlag_covariance — Function
rumschlag_covariance(fit) -> Matrix{Float64}

Asymptotic covariance matrix of the log parameters of a maximum_likelihood fit of a RumschlagModel, in the order of parameter_names. It is the inverse of minus a central finite-difference Hessian of the log-likelihood, with step h on the log scale.

source
Chytridiomycosis.rumschlag_summary — Function
rumschlag_summary(fit; ZL0_infection = 1e5) -> NamedTuple

The quantities that Rumschlag et al. (2022) report, computed from a maximum_likelihood fit and its rumschlag_covariance:

  • lifespan: the expected lifespan 24/m (hours) of each water, by water;
  • persistence: the expected time 24/d (hours) from death to decomposition, by water;
  • β: the transmission rate on the paper's scale, β ZL0 / ZL0_infection.

Each value is a (estimate, halfwidth) pair. The paper writes its 95% intervals as "estimate ± halfwidth". These are reproduced when the halfwidth is half the width of the Wald interval of the rate (m ± 1.96 se), carried to 24/m hours.

The paper's β does not match β in this package's parametrization. The infection data identify only the product β ZL0. The paper's value (7.1 × 10⁻⁶) equals that product divided by 10⁵ to two figures. So the paper appears to have used a fixed initial concentration of 10⁵ (units unstated) in eq. 10 for the infection assay. ZL0_infection = 1e5 is that inferred convention, not a value printed in the paper.
