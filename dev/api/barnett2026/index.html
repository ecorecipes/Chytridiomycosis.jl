Imperfect prophylaxis ABM (Barnett et al. 2026, preprint)

Preprint

This model comes from a bioRxiv preprint that has not been peer reviewed: Barnett et al. (2026), "From protection to amplification: imperfect chytridiomycosis prophylaxis increases infections in wild amphibians", doi:10.64898/2026.05.15.725113 (v2, August 2026). The authors' NetLogo code was not released, so this implementation was checked against the text only.

The preprint couples a field trial with an agent-based model. In the field trial, Bd metabolites (a prophylaxis that acts like an imperfect vaccine) were added to whole ponds in California. The agent-based model asks how such an intervention changes Bd in a cohort of Pacific chorus frogs (Pseudacris regilla). It follows tadpoles over a 90-day season, through metamorphosis, on a pond made of three kinds of patch: perimeter (shallow), deep and terrestrial. Each host carries a zoosporangium load. Zoospores pass between hosts through the perimeter patches, and hosts also reinfect themselves. Natural mortality is daily and stage-specific. A metamorph dies of Bd when its load reaches smax = 562.

A vaccinated tadpole has one or more of its traits changed:

ModeTrait changedProtective direction
anti-infection resistance (:infection)establishment probability estdecrease
anti-transmission resistance (:transmission)zoospores shed per zoosporangiumdecrease
anti-growth resistance (:growth)daily clearance of zoosporangiaincrease
tolerance (:tolerance)lethal load smaxincrease

The preprint's "adverse reaction" scenarios reverse each direction (barnett_vaccine(...; adverse = true)). Every published value (Table S1 and Methods) is in data/compendium/barnett2026.toml.

Choices where the preprint is silent

The source header of src/models/barnett2026.jl lists every choice. The ones that matter most are these:

  • Population and pond size. The preprint gives neither. The package uses 1000 tadpoles, so that 0.2% initially infected is 2 tadpoles, and a ring of 60 perimeter patches.
  • Transmission. The preprint describes a multinomial draw driven by an exposure parameter (0.25) and zoospore mortality (2 day⁻¹). The package reads these as competing daily hazards.
  • Vaccine efficacy. A change of e is applied as the factor (1 ± e)^imm, which is the preprint's exponential modulation. Here imm is uniform on 1 ± 10%.
  • The intensity statistic. The preprint does not define it. The default (metric = :metamorph) is the season mean of the mean load of infected live metamorphs. :season uses all live hosts, and :final uses the survivors on the last day.

What is reproduced

The targets in data/validation/targets.toml test the signs of the preprint's percent changes against an unvaccinated control.

The model reproduces the preprint's headline results:

  • Resistance (anti-infection or anti-transmission) lowers infection intensity and increases the final population.
  • Tolerance raises infection intensity. This is the amplification that the preprint offers as one explanation of its field result.
  • Increased susceptibility, an adverse reaction, raises intensity and lowers the population. This is the second explanation. The intensity effect is only weak with the default statistic.
  • Reduced tolerance lowers intensity.

Four statements are discrepant:

  • Anti-growth resistance does not lower the metamorph intensity.
  • Resistance does not counteract tolerance in the two-way scenarios (Fig. 4a, Fig. S7).
  • The control's median prevalence is 0, not 100%.

Self-reinfection (17.8 × 0.1 × 0.25 ≈ 0.45 per zoosporangium per day) outgrows clearance even when clearance is doubled. As a result, infected tadpoles reach the lethal load by metamorphosis. With the season-mean statistic, the growth and two-way results have the preprint's sign, but tolerance alone no longer does. The field-trial numbers are pending, because the field data are not deposited.

Chytridiomycosis.BarnettParameters — Type
BarnettParameters(; tad_mort, meta_mort, t_movement, m_land, meta_start, meta_prob,
    meta_end, last_day, init_prevalence, init_load, exposure, est, z_mort,
    maturation_days, clearance, shedding, shed_own, shed_neighbour, self_reinfection,
    smax, s_k, v_efficacy, relative_variation, n_tadpoles = 1000, n_perimeter = 60,
    perimeter_from_perimeter = 0.25, perimeter_from_land = 0.375)
BarnettParameters(:barnett2026; kwargs...)

Parameters of the Barnett et al. (2026, bioRxiv preprint) agent-based model of Bd in a cohort of Pseudacris regilla tadpoles and metamorphs. Rates and probabilities are per day.

  • tad_mort, meta_mort: daily probabilities of natural death;
  • t_movement: fraction of tadpoles moving to another perimeter patch each day;
  • m_land: fraction of metamorphs on land each day;
  • meta_start, meta_prob, meta_end: metamorphosis window and daily probability;
  • last_day: number of daily ticks;
  • init_prevalence, init_load: fraction of tadpoles infected at the start, and their load (zoosporangia);
  • exposure: per-host daily contact hazard with a zoospore on the same patch; est: establishment probability per contacting zoospore; z_mort: zoospore death rate;
  • maturation_days: prezoosporangium stages before a zoosporangium;
  • clearance: daily clearance probability of a zoosporangium; shedding: zoospores per zoosporangium per day; shed_own, shed_neighbour, self_reinfection: shares of shed zoospores to the host's patch, a neighbouring patch and the host itself;
  • smax: lethal load of a metamorph; s_k: tadpole load above which no new infections establish;
  • v_efficacy, relative_variation: a vaccinated host's response imm is uniform on v_efficacy × [1 − relative_variation, 1 + relative_variation].

The last four keywords are not in the preprint and are package choices: the initial number of tadpoles, the number of perimeter pond patches (a ring), and the probabilities that the neighbouring share of shed zoospores reaches the perimeter ring from a perimeter patch (2 of 8 neighbours) and from a terrestrial patch (3 of 8).

The keyed constructor reads every published value from data/compendium/barnett2026.toml.

Chytridiomycosis.BarnettVaccine — Type
BarnettVaccine(; coverage = 0, infection = 0, transmission = 0, growth = 0,
    tolerance = 0)

An imperfect vaccine (prophylaxis) in the Barnett et al. (2026, preprint) model. coverage is the fraction of tadpoles selected on day 0. The other four are signed relative changes ("efficacy") to a vaccinated host's baseline trait:

  • infection: change to the establishment probability est (anti-infection resistance is negative, for example -0.3 for a 30% decrease);
  • transmission: change to the shedding rate (anti-transmission resistance is negative);
  • growth: change to the clearance probability (anti-growth resistance is positive);
  • tolerance: change to the lethal load smax (tolerance is positive).

A change e enters as the constant c = log(1 + e) of the preprint's exponential modulation x0 exp(c imm); each must be at least -1. Adverse reactions (the preprint's "hypothetical adverse reactions") use the opposite signs. See barnett_vaccine.

Chytridiomycosis.barnett_vaccine — Function
barnett_vaccine(mode, efficacy; coverage, adverse = false) -> BarnettVaccine
barnett_vaccine(modes::Pair...; coverage, adverse = false) -> BarnettVaccine

The vaccine of one of the preprint's scenarios. mode is one of :infection (decreases establishment), :transmission (decreases shedding), :growth (increases clearance) or :tolerance (increases the lethal load), and efficacy the size of the change (0.1–1.0 in the preprint). With adverse = true the direction is reversed (increased establishment, increased shedding, decreased clearance, decreased tolerance). Pairs such as :tolerance => 0.8, :growth => 0.2 combine modes (the two-way scenarios, at the coverage coverage_two_way = 0.75 of the compendium).

Chytridiomycosis.BarnettABMProblem — Type
BarnettABMProblem(p::BarnettParameters, vaccine = BarnettVaccine(); save_daily = false)

One run of the Barnett et al. (2026, bioRxiv preprint) agent-based model, from day 0 to p.last_day. solve(prob; rng) returns a NamedTuple:

  • survivors: live hosts at the end of the last day;
  • season means over the days (end of each day; days where a quantity is undefined are skipped): prevalence (fraction of live hosts with a zoosporangium load above 0), intensity (mean load of infected live hosts), unvaccinated_intensity (the same for unvaccinated hosts), metamorph_prevalence, metamorph_intensity, metamorph_unvaccinated_intensity (the same among metamorphs) and zoospore_density (zoospores per perimeter patch);
  • the same at the end of the last day, among survivors: final_prevalence, final_intensity, final_unvaccinated_intensity, final_zoospore_density (NaN when undefined);
  • vaccinated_survivors, unvaccinated_survivors, n_vaccinated (hosts vaccinated on day 0);
  • loads, vaccinated: load and vaccination status of each survivor;
  • with save_daily = true, also day, tadpoles, metamorphs, infected and zoospores (total on perimeter patches) at the end of each day.

Chytridiomycosis.barnett_relative_change — Function
barnett_relative_change(p, vaccine; metric = :metamorph, nreps, ncontrol, rng,
    control = nothing) -> NamedTuple

Percent change of a vaccination scenario relative to the unvaccinated control, as in the preprint's figures: 100 (scenario mean − control mean) / control mean for survivors (final population size), prevalence, intensity, zoospore_density and unvaccinated_intensity (infected unvaccinated hosts against all infected hosts of the control, Fig. 2a–b). The preprint does not define these statistics, so three readings are offered: metric = :metamorph (default) uses season means over metamorphs (prevalence among live metamorphs, mean load of infected live metamorphs), the stage the field trial sampled and the only stage that dies of Bd; :season uses season means over all live hosts; :final uses the survivors at the end of the last day. Zoospore density is the season mean for :metamorph and :season. Defaults: nreps = n_reps = 25 scenario runs and ncontrol = n_control = 250 control runs (compendium). Pass a precomputed control (barnett_summary of the unvaccinated model) to reuse it across scenarios.

unvaccinated_survival is the percent change in the unvaccinated share of survivors relative to the unvaccinated share at the start, 1 − coverage (Fig. 2c–d).
