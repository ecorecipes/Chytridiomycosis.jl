Imperfect prophylaxis ABM (Barnett et al. 2026, preprint)

Preprint

This model comes from a bioRxiv preprint that has not been peer reviewed: Barnett et al. (2026), "From protection to amplification: imperfect chytridiomycosis prophylaxis increases infections in wild amphibians", doi:10.64898/2026.05.15.725113 (v2, August 2026). The agent-based model is a port of the authors' NetLogo code (kmbarnett100/protectiontoamplification), checked against all their released runs.

The preprint couples a field trial with an agent-based model. In the field trial, Bd metabolites (a prophylaxis that acts like an imperfect vaccine) were added to whole ponds in California. The agent-based model asks how such an intervention changes Bd in a cohort of Pacific chorus frogs (Pseudacris regilla). It follows tadpoles over a 90-day season, through metamorphosis, on a pond made of three kinds of patch: perimeter (shallow), deep and terrestrial. Each host carries a zoosporangium load. Zoospores pass between hosts through the perimeter patches, and hosts also reinfect themselves. Natural mortality is daily and stage-specific. A metamorph dies of Bd when its load reaches smax = 562.

A vaccinated tadpole has one or more of its traits changed:

ModeTrait changedProtective direction
anti-infection resistance (:infection)establishment probability estdecrease
anti-transmission resistance (:transmission)zoospores shed per zoosporangiumdecrease
anti-growth resistance (:growth)daily clearance of zoosporangiaincrease
tolerance (:tolerance)lethal load smaxincrease

The preprint's "adverse reaction" scenarios reverse each direction (barnett_vaccine(...; adverse = true)). Every value (Table S1, Methods and the NetLogo code) is in data/compendium/barnett2026.toml.

The code and the text

The port follows the code where it differs from the preprint's text. The source header of src/models/barnett2026.jl lists every difference. The ones that matter most are these:

  • Infection. Zoospores on a patch are shared among all hosts in the world, not only those on the patch. Only the share that falls on hosts of that patch infects them. A vaccinated host's lower establishment probability reduces the number of establishing zoospores for everybody, but not that host's own share.
  • Clearance is a rate: daily survival of a zoosporangium is $e^{-0.2} = 0.82$.
  • Stalled infections. A host whose zoosporangia number 0 stops maturing the stages it carries until it is infected again.
  • Population. Every perimeter patch starts with 500 tadpoles, one infected. Ponds have about 136 patches, and 28–49 of them are on the perimeter.
  • Vaccine response varies by ±5%, not ±10%, and not at all in the two-way runs.
  • Statistics. Intensity is the median load of infected metamorphs on the last day, and changes are relative to the median control run. Runs with fewer than two infected metamorphs are left out.

What is reproduced

scripts/barnett2026_abm.jl runs the package with the parameters of each of the authors' 29,325 runs, eight times over, and compares the distributions (data/barnett2026/). NetLogo's random numbers cannot be reproduced, so the match is in distribution.

Of 85 comparisons of means (up to nine outputs per file), 77 have |z| < 2 and one has |z| > 3: the survivors of the increased-shedding runs (42.9 against 48.5, z = 3.6), recorded as discrepant. That gap does not grow with efficacy, and is not explained. The adverse-tolerance file shows smaller differences in the same direction (|z| up to 2.9). The other ten files agree within sampling error.

Every statement of the preprint about its scenario figures that the package checks has the published sign, by a clear margin. This includes three that an earlier version of this page recorded as discrepant, when only the text was available.

Increased susceptibility

The preprint reports that infection intensity rises when vaccination increases susceptibility (Fig. 5a). That holds where metamorphs survive (about +25% at 50% efficacy and coverage). At high efficacy and coverage almost none do (none of the authors' 75 runs at +100% and coverage of at least 80%), so that corner of the figure is extrapolated.

Field trial and challenge experiment

BarnettBACIModel refits the authors' four mixed models (zero-inflated negative binomial for intensity, binomial for prevalence; pond and year as random effects), with the Laplace likelihood that glmmTMB uses. Log-likelihoods, estimates, standard errors and marginal means agree with glmmTMB to four or five digits. That includes the headline interaction (P = 0.00135) and the 88% and 23% declines. The preprint's text prints the intervals of the two control-pond prevalences swapped. The ratio it gives as 6.42 is 6.44 before rounding.

Data not bundled

The repository has no licence, so its files are not bundled. barnett2026_path finds them in the lab workspace or in ENV["CHYTRIDIOMYCOSIS_BARNETT2026"].

API

Chytridiomycosis.BarnettParameters — Type
BarnettParameters(:barnett2026; kwargs...)

Parameters of the Barnett et al. (2026, bioRxiv preprint) agent-based model of Bd in a cohort of Pseudacris regilla tadpoles and metamorphs, read from data/compendium/barnett2026.toml (values of the preprint and of the authors' NetLogo code). Rates and probabilities are per day.

  • tad_mort, meta_mort: daily probabilities of natural death;
  • t_movement: fraction of tadpoles moving to a random perimeter patch each day;
  • m_land: fraction of metamorphs on land each day;
  • meta_start, meta_prob, meta_end: a tadpole older than meta_start days metamorphoses with daily probability meta_prob; all do once one is older than meta_end;
  • last_day: number of daily ticks;
  • tadpoles_per_patch, infected_per_patch, init_load: tadpoles and infected tadpoles on each perimeter patch at the start, and the zoosporangia of each infected one;
  • exposure, est, z_mort: exposure per host, establishment probability, zoospore death rate;
  • clearance: zoosporangium clearance rate (daily survival exp(−clearance));
  • shedding: zoospores per zoosporangium per day; self_reinfection, shed_own, shed_neighbour: the share that re-exposes the host, the share of the rest that goes to the tadpole's patch, and the share of what is left that goes to a neighbouring perimeter patch;
  • smax: lethal load of a metamorph; s_k: tadpole load above which no new infections establish;
  • v_efficacy, relative_variation: a vaccinated host's response imm is uniform on v_efficacy × [1 − relative_variation/2, 1 + relative_variation/2];
  • natural_imm: increase in imm on clearing an infection (0 in every published run);
  • world_size, pond_mean, pond_sd: side of the square world and the normal distribution of the pond size, in patches.

Chytridiomycosis.BarnettVaccine — Type
BarnettVaccine(; coverage = 0, c_est = 0, c_shedding = 0, c_clear = 0, c_smax = 0)

An imperfect vaccine (prophylaxis) in the Barnett et al. (2026, preprint) model. coverage is the fraction of tadpoles selected on the first day, and the constants c multiply a vaccinated host's traits by exp(c imm): establishment probability (c_est), shedding (c_shedding), clearance rate (c_clear) and lethal load (c_smax). See barnett_vaccine for the preprint's scenarios.

Chytridiomycosis.barnett_vaccine — Function
barnett_vaccine(mode, efficacy; coverage, adverse = false) -> BarnettVaccine
barnett_vaccine(modes::Pair...; coverage, adverse = false) -> BarnettVaccine

The vaccine of one of the preprint's scenarios. mode is one of :infection (decreases establishment), :transmission (decreases shedding), :growth (increases clearance) or :tolerance (increases the lethal load), and efficacy the size of the change (0.1–1.0 in the preprint), converted to the authors' constants (log(1 ± e) rounded to 3 decimals; −0.11 for a 10% and −16.118 for a 100% decrease). With adverse = true the direction is reversed (the "hypothetical adverse reactions"). Pairs such as :tolerance => 0.8, :growth => 0.2 combine modes; the authors' two-way runs used coverage_two_way = 0.75 and no variation between hosts (relative_variation = 0).

Chytridiomycosis.BarnettABMProblem — Type
BarnettABMProblem(p::BarnettParameters, vaccine = BarnettVaccine(); save_daily = false)

One run of the Barnett et al. (2026, bioRxiv preprint) agent-based model, a port of the authors' NetLogo code. solve(prob; rng) returns the authors' BehaviorSpace outputs at the end of the last day, as a NamedTuple:

  • survivors: metamorphs alive (count metamorphs);
  • prevalence, unvaccinated_prevalence, vaccinated_prevalence: fraction infected;
  • median_intensity, mean_intensity, and the same with the prefix unvaccinated_ or vaccinated_: load of metamorphs with zoosporangia (at least two of them);
  • unvaccinated_fraction: fraction of survivors that are unvaccinated;
  • zoospore_density: sum over days of the zoospores per perimeter patch;
  • bd_death_fraction: share of metamorph deaths due to Bd; realized_coverage: fraction of tadpoles immunised; n_perimeter: number of perimeter patches;
  • with save_daily = true, also day, tadpoles, metamorphs, infected and zoospores (on perimeter patches) at the end of each day.

Quantities the authors code as missing (−100; 0 in their older runs) are NaN.

barnett_summary(p::BarnettParameters, vaccine = BarnettVaccine(); nreps, rng) -> NamedTuple

Run nreps replicates (default n_reps = 25 from the compendium) of BarnettABMProblem. Returns runs (a NamedTuple of vectors, one per output), mean and median (over the runs where an output is defined, as the authors' figure code does) and dieoff, the fraction of runs with no metamorph left.

Chytridiomycosis.barnett_relative_change — Function
barnett_relative_change(p, vaccine; nreps, ncontrol, rng, control = nothing) -> NamedTuple

Percent change of a vaccination scenario relative to the unvaccinated control, as in the authors' figure code: each run's output minus the median of the control runs, divided by that median, times 100, averaged over the runs where it is defined. (The authors smooth these run-level values over efficacy and coverage with a GAM; the package reports the mean of the cell.) Returns survivors (final population), intensity (median load of infected metamorphs), prevalence, zoospore_density, unvaccinated_intensity (Fig. 2a–b), unvaccinated_survival (unvaccinated share of the survivors against 1 − coverage, Fig. 2c–d), and the summaries scenario and control.

Defaults: nreps = n_reps = 25 scenario runs and ncontrol = n_control = 250 control runs (compendium). Pass a precomputed control (barnett_summary of the unvaccinated model) to reuse it across scenarios.

Chytridiomycosis.load_barnett2026 — Function
load_barnett2026(; path = barnett2026_path()) -> (field, challenge)

Chytridiomycosis.BarnettBACIModel — Type
BarnettBACIModel(kind; data = load_barnett2026())

One of the four BACI mixed models of Barnett et al. (2026, bioRxiv preprint): kind is :field_intensity, :field_prevalence, :challenge_intensity or :challenge_prevalence. Intensity models are zero-inflated negative binomial (NB2, log link, the same predictors in the zero-inflation part); prevalence models are binomial (logit). Fixed effects are treatment × time; the group effects are pond and year (field) or pond (challenge). Fit with maximum_likelihood; the log-likelihood is the Laplace approximation used by glmmTMB.

Parameters, in order: the four fixed effects (intercept = sham after, vaccine, before, interaction) and the log standard deviations of the group effects of the count (or binomial) part; for intensity models, the same for the zero-inflation part, then the log dispersion.

Chytridiomycosis.maximum_likelihood — Method
maximum_likelihood(m::BarnettBACIModel; x0, iters = 400) -> NamedTuple

Maximum (Laplace) likelihood by BFGS with central-difference gradients. Returns loglik, x (in the order of parameter_names(m)), estimates, se, interaction (estimate, SE, z and two-sided P of the treatment × time term of the count or binomial part), gradient (largest absolute gradient at the maximum) and means: for sham_after, sham_before, vaccine_after and vaccine_before, the marginal mean on the link scale (link), its se, the 95% interval on the link scale (lower, upper) and the back-transformed response, response_lower and response_upper.
