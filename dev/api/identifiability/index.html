Swabs, eDNA and identifiability

New model (N5)

This observation model and the identifiability results are new work in this package, not published results. The assay scales used in examples are illustrative assumptions.

An observation model that links the Briggs et al. (2010) load model (doi:10.1073/pnas.0912886107) to skin-swab qPCR (per frog, or pooled) and to eDNA in pond water, and tools to ask which Briggs parameters each data stream can recover.

In the linear (sub-lethal) phase, the mean sporangial load per frog m, the zoospore pool Z and the excess variance of load among frogs w = Var − m obey a linear system (BriggsMomentProblem); k = m²/w is the aggregation of loads (negative-binomial shape, as in LoadBDI). A swab tube of n pooled swabs is negative binomial with shape n k and mean n ρ m; an eDNA sample is lognormal around κ Z/V.

What each design identifies (structural, by hand and confirmed by sensitivity_rank):

DataIdentified
swab means, one densitytrace and determinant of the linear system only
swab means (or eDNA alone), two or more densitiesγ/V, μ, r0 = ηνf − σ, νη(1−f)
+ eDNA, both assays on absolute scalesν and η(1−f)
+ aggregation k from individual swabsηf, so every parameter

Without an absolute swab scale (copies per sporangium), the symmetry ρ → cρ, ν → ν/c remains. At one density, γ and μ are never separated. expected_information measures how well a design does in practice; fits use maximum_likelihood and profile_likelihood, which have methods for SurveyModel, and parameter_names.

Latent moments

Chytridiomycosis.BriggsMomentProblem — Type
BriggsMomentProblem(p::BriggsParameters; N, Z0, m0 = 0.0, w0 = 0.0, tspan = (0.0, 60.0))

Moments of the sporangial load on N frogs sharing one pool, in the linear (sub-lethal) phase of the Briggs et al. (2010) model with each frog's load a birth–death–immigration process given the pool (see LoadBDI): the mean m (sporangia per frog), the pool Z (zoospores) and w = Var − m, the excess variance of load among frogs. Z0 zoospores start in the pool; m0, w0 set the initial moments (default: frogs free of Bd).

solve(prob; saveat) is exact (a matrix exponential) and returns a NamedTuple with vectors t, m, Z, w and the aggregation k = m²/w, the negative-binomial shape of loads among frogs. Smax is ignored: use it only while loads stay well below Smax. This is new work (model N5), not a published result.

Design, data and likelihood

Chytridiomycosis.SurveyPond — Type
SurveyPond(; N, days, swabs = 0, pool = 1, edna = 0)

Sampling design for one pond of N frogs: on each of days (after the pool is seeded on day 0), swabs qPCR results from skin swabs, each from pool frogs' swabs combined in one tube (pool = 1: individual swabs; swabs * pool ≤ N), and edna water samples.

source
Chytridiomycosis.SurveySample — Type
SurveySample(pond, day, kind, pool, copies)

One qPCR result: pond index, day, kind (:swab or :edna), the number of swabs pool combined in the tube (1 for eDNA), and copies (Bd ITS copies per swab tube, or per eDNA sample; eDNA values below 1 copy are non-detections).

source
Chytridiomycosis.SurveyObservation — Type
SurveyObservation(; ρ, κ, σe)

Assay scales and noise: a swab carries on average ρ Bd ITS copies per sporangium on the frog (copies sporangium⁻¹); an eDNA sample carries on average κ copies per unit of zoospore density Z/V (copies per sample per zoospore volume-unit⁻¹, i.e. copies per zoospore times the volume filtered); σe is the standard deviation of log eDNA copies. None of these has a published value for the Briggs model: they must be supplied (or fitted), and stated.

source
Chytridiomycosis.simulate_survey — Function
simulate_survey(rng, p::BriggsParameters, obs::SurveyObservation, ponds; Z0)
    -> Vector{SurveySample}

Synthetic data for the designs ponds (a vector of SurveyPond), with Z0[i] zoospores seeded into pond i on day 0. Swab tubes are negative binomial with shape pool·k and mean pool·ρ·m (the sum of pool frogs); eDNA is lognormal around κZ/V, with values below 1 copy recorded as 0. For identifiability studies only: the output of a new model.

source
Chytridiomycosis.SurveyModel — Type
SurveyModel(samples, ponds; p::BriggsParameters, obs::SurveyObservation, Z0,
    free = (:γ, :ν, :f, :μ, :η, :σ, :ρ, :κ, :σe, :Z0_1, …))

Likelihood of swab and eDNA samples from the ponds designs (see simulate_survey) under the Briggs moment model (new model N5). The parameters are the Briggs rates γ, ν, f, μ, η, σ (V and Smax are fixed from p), the assay scales ρ, κ, σe and the seeded pool Z0_i of each pond. Those in free are estimated on the log scale; the rest are fixed at the values in p, obs and Z0, which also give the starting point. Fixing κ means an eDNA assay on an absolute scale, fixing ρ a swab assay calibrated in sporangia.

Implements LogDensityProblems (log-likelihood of the log parameters, flat prior, in the order of parameter_names); fit with maximum_likelihood and profile_likelihood; check identifiability with sensitivity_rank, expected_information and observed_information.

Identifiability

Chytridiomycosis.identifiable_combinations — Function
identifiable_combinations(p::BriggsParameters)

The combinations of Briggs parameters that the N5 hand derivation shows are identified by richer and richer data (new work, not a published result):

  • γ_V = γ/V, μ and r0 = ηνf − σ: swab means (or eDNA) at two or more host densities, from the trace r0 − μ − γN/V and determinant −r0(μ + γN/V) − νγη(1−f)N/V of the linear system; at one density only the trace and determinant are identified;
  • νη_1mf = νη(1−f): the same data (slope of the determinant in N);
  • ν and η(1−f) separately: add eDNA, with both assays on absolute scales (otherwise only ρν/κ);
  • ηf: add the aggregation of loads among frogs, k = γZ/(Vηf), with the pool Z on an absolute scale; then η, f and σ follow.

See the wiki page models/new-swab-edna-identifiability.md.

source
Chytridiomycosis.sensitivity_rank — Function
sensitivity_rank(m::SurveyModel, x = log of the starting values; aggregation = true,
    rtol = 1e-7)

Local structural identifiability of the free parameters of m under its sampling design, from the rank of the Jacobian of the noise-free outputs (log swab mean ρm, log aggregation k = m²/w if aggregation, log eDNA level κZ/V, at every sampled pond and day) with respect to the log parameters, computed exactly by complex steps. aggregation = false describes swab totals (pooled samples whose spread is not used). The data are not used.

Returns a NamedTuple: rank, nparams, singular (singular values, largest first), names, and null, a vector of NamedTuples (parameter name ⇒ log-scale component, the largest scaled to ±1), one per unidentified direction: moving along it leaves every output unchanged. The null space is a basis, so with several directions any mixture of them is also unidentified.

source
Chytridiomycosis.expected_information — Function
expected_information(m::SurveyModel, x = log of the starting values; aggregation = true)

Expected Fisher information of the free log parameters of m for its sampling design (m.ponds; the samples are not used), at log parameters x. Each swab tube contributes through its negative-binomial mean (information rM/(r+M) for log M) and, if aggregation, its shape (computed by summation); each eDNA sample through its log level (1/σe²; censoring below one copy is ignored) and log σe (2). aggregation = false gives the information in swab totals alone. sqrt.(diag(inv(I))) are the smallest attainable standard errors on the log scale (≈ coefficients of variation): use it to compare designs.

source
Chytridiomycosis.observed_information — Function
observed_information(m::SurveyModel, x = log of the starting values; h = 1e-3)

Observed Fisher information (minus the Hessian of the log-likelihood, central differences with step h) at log parameters x. Its inverse approximates the covariance of the log estimates, so sqrt.(diag(inv(I))) are standard errors on the log scale (≈ coefficients of variation); a near-singular matrix flags a practically unidentified direction.
