Chytridiomycosis

Models of amphibian chytridiomycosis (Batrachochytrium dendrobatidis, Bd, and B. salamandrivorans, Bsal) in three categories: reference implementations of published models, checked against the papers by validation targets; modified versions of published models; and new models. Results from the last two are not published results. Published parameter values come from a compendium that records units and provenance.

source

Models of amphibian chytridiomycosis, caused by the fungi Batrachochytrium dendrobatidis (Bd) and B. salamandrivorans (Bsal), built on SciML. The package contains reference implementations of published models, a parameter compendium that records where every published value comes from, modified versions of published models, and new models, including the treatment of amphibians with protective skin bacteria.

Every model belongs to one of three categories. They differ in what a result from them means:

  1. Published models reimplement a paper's equations. The only departures are documented choices where the paper is silent, and switches between what was printed and what the authors coded (as_printed, as_coded). Their results are checked against the papers by 352 validation targets. Some are not reproduced, and those are recorded as discrepant.
  2. Modified published models add structure the paper did not have: a term, a stage, a stochastic version, a geometry. Their results are not published results.
  3. New models are new structure, built on top of published or modified host models. Their results are not published results, and most of their added parameter values are illustrative.

Shared infrastructure

These are not models of any one paper: the parameter compendium, units of loads and rates, thermal performance curves, validation targets, generic integral projection model, hidden Markov model and multistate capture–recapture machinery, qPCR load units and likelihoods, and glue to DynamicalResilience.jl.

Getting started

using Chytridiomycosis
p = SchmidtParameters(:schmidt2017)      # values from the compendium
basic_reproduction_number(p)             # 24.8
sol = solve(SchmidtSLIProblem(p))        # an ODE solution

Every problem is a struct describing the model and its set-up, solved with solve. Most return a named tuple of time series and summaries; see each problem's documentation.

Tutorials

Every tutorial opens with a table of the models it uses and the category of each.

Part I. Published models

  1. Can a Bsal outbreak be stopped?: five published Bsal models, from transmission and host density to seasons, life stages, temperature, load and management.
  2. Does it matter that frogs carry different Bd loads?: from the mean load to load distributions and integral projection models fitted to swabs.
  3. Does chance decide whether frogs survive Bd?: whole frogs and whole zoospores, in a frog population and in outdoor mesocosms.
  4. Where does Bd live between hosts?: zoospores in water, several host species sharing one pool, and temperature.
  5. What do field data say about Bd?: swab loads, outbreak states of lakes, and survival of infected frogs with imperfect detection.
  6. Thresholds and resilience: how robust a state is on the way to a threshold.

Part II. Modified and new models

  1. Does a protective skin microbiome save the population?: for three systems, the treatment model is built from the simplest model one piece of biology at a time, ending with chance.
  2. One model, many switches: a single nested model in which every combination of biology is compared like for like, and a stochastic version.
  3. Fitting to a field trial: the frog load equation fitted to field swabs of treated, wild and control frogs, comparing four explanations for why treated frogs stayed low.
  4. Does it matter that frogs carry different amounts of a protective bacterium?: a two-trait IPM of Bd load and J. lividum load, fitted to the joint loads of the treated frogs of a field trial, and how the spread of bacterial loads and the kind of protection change survival and R0.

The tutorials are Quarto documents in vignettes/, rendered to HTML, PDF and Markdown.
