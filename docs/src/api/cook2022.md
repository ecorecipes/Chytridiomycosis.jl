# Treatment of metamorphs and population projection (Cook 2022)

```@meta
CurrentModule = Chytridiomycosis
```

This is a reference implementation of the stage-structured population projection of Cook et
al. (2022), *Conserv Sci Pract* 4:e12762
([doi:10.1111/csp2.12762](https://doi.org/10.1111/csp2.12762)). The study treated Cascades
frog (*Rana cascadae*) metamorphs with itraconazole, which raised their apparent overwinter
survival from 0.11 to 0.48. The projection asks what this does to population growth. The
model is described only in the Supporting Information ("Population simulations", Figure S3,
Table S2), and every value comes from the compendium.

## Model

Each time step is one year. The stages are metamorphs, young of the previous year (YOPY),
subadults and adults.

- **Metamorphs.** They survive their first winter with probability `SU` if untreated or `ST`
  if treated (posterior means 0.12 and 0.49).
- **YOPY.** They survive with probability `SY = 0.8 SA`.
- **Subadults.** They survive with probability `SS = SA`. Survivors mature with probability
  `GS = 0.725`.
- **Adults.** They survive with probability `SA`.
- **Reproduction.** Adult females (a fraction `PF = 0.52` of adults) each produce
  `Poisson(F)` metamorphs.

Each year, `SA` and `F` are drawn from the 28 population-year estimates of Table S2
(environmental stochasticity). All transitions are binomial (demographic stochasticity). A
simulation starts from 1500 metamorphs, 50 YOPY, 50 subadults and 100 adults and runs for 10
years. Its `λ` is the geometric mean of the annual adult growth rates. The paper reports the
mean over 1000 simulations.

!!! note "Choices where the SI is silent"
    The authors' code was not found. Each choice below has a switch in
    [`CookProjectionProblem`](@ref) or [`CookParameters`](@ref).

    - The metamorphs of a year come from the adults present that year, after overwinter
      survival (`breeding = :after_survival`).
    - Surviving subadults mature with probability `GS`.
    - `SA` and `F` are drawn from the same population-year (`paired = true`).
    - `SU` and `ST` are fixed at their posterior means, because the posteriors are not
      published.
    - Treatment years are a fixed share of the 10 years, placed at random
      (`schedule = :fixed`).

## Results

- **Untreated.** Mean `λ` is 0.94, which reproduces the published 0.9.
- **40% of metamorphs treated in 40% of years.** Mean `λ` is 1.02, above 1 as published.
  Lowering either share to 20% gives `λ < 1`.
- **All metamorphs treated every year.** Mean `λ` is 1.30, against the published 1.2. This
  target is discrepant. Breeding before survival gives 0.97 and 1.25 instead.

The projection matrix of mean rates ([`cook_projection_matrix`](@ref)) supports sensitivity
and elasticity analysis. The paper prints neither, and the matrix's `λ` is not a published
number.
